Our Sweet Journey

How a simple passion for crafting fresh organic treats turned into a dream shared across generations.

2018

The First Churn

Starting out of a cozy garage workshop with just a single vintage hand-crank machine, crafting 5 classic flavor varieties for local neighborhood farm gatherings.

2021

Going Organic

We built permanent partnerships with local sustainable fruit orchards and organic dairy meadows, completely eliminating artificial sweeteners from our entire recipe lineup.

2024

Opening Scoops Hub

Launched our primary boutique ice cream parlor flagship store, creating an experiential sweet getaway featuring our iconic floating 3-scoop visual cone arrangements.

2026

Digital Dreams Club

Expanding our community network online with automated custom order tracking options and rewarding rewards tracking tiers built natively for ice cream lovers everywhere.
